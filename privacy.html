Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026

Candle Lab ("Candle Lab", "we", "us") is a mobile app that helps candle makers track supplies, recipes, production batches, and sales. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and the choices you have.

The short version: we collect only what's needed to run your account and store the business records you enter. We don't use advertising, we don't track you across apps or websites, and we never sell your data.

Information we collect

Account information

  • Email address, used to create your account, sign you in, and contact you about your account.
  • Password, which is stored only in hashed (encrypted) form by our authentication provider. We can't see it.

Business data you enter

Everything you add to the app is stored in your account so it's available whenever you sign in:

  • Inventory and supplies (names, types, quantities, units, costs, low-stock thresholds)
  • Recipes and their ingredients
  • Production batches and notes
  • Sales (product, quantity, price, sales channel, date, notes)
  • Finished-candle shelf stock

This data belongs to you. We use it only to provide the app's features to you.

Subscription information

If you subscribe to Candle Lab Pro, payment is handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never receive your card number or billing details. Our subscription provider, RevenueCat, receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase and subscription status from Apple or Google so the app knows whether Pro is active.

Stored on your device only

A few preferences are saved locally on your phone and are not sent to us: your light/dark mode choice, whether you've completed onboarding, and an in-progress Market Mode session.

What we don't collect

Candle Lab does not access your location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone. The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking tools.

Website calculators

The free calculators on this website run entirely in your browser. The numbers you type are saved only in your own browser (so you don't have to retype them) and are never sent to us. The website uses no cookies, advertising, or analytics.

How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in
  • To store and sync your business records and show you totals, profit, and analytics
  • To unlock Pro features when you have an active subscription
  • To respond when you contact support
  • To keep the service secure and working properly

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

Service providers

We use a small number of trusted providers to run the app. They process data only on our behalf:

  • Supabase for account sign-in and secure database hosting of your data.
  • RevenueCat for managing subscription status.
  • Apple and Google for app distribution and payment processing, under their own privacy policies.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users or the service.

Data security

Data is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Each account can access only its own records; database rules prevent other users from reading or changing your data. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data for as long as your account exists.

You can delete your account at any time from inside the app: open the menu, tap your profile, then tap Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and all of your data (inventory, recipes, batches, sales, and shelf stock) right away. It can't be undone.

You can also ask us to delete your account by emailing candlelab.support@gmail.com from the email address on your account.

Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription. Please cancel it in your device's subscription settings so you aren't charged again.

Your choices and rights

  • Access and export: view all your data in the app at any time, and Pro users can export sales, batches, and inventory as CSV files.
  • Correction: edit any record in the app.
  • Deletion: delete individual records or your entire account in the app, as described above.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA. To make a request, contact us at the email below.

Children

Candle Lab is a business tool intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. We'll update the effective date above, and for significant changes we'll let you know in the app or by email.

Contact

Questions or requests about privacy? Email candlelab.support@gmail.com.
