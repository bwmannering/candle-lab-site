Email candlelab.support@gmail.com and we'll get back to you, usually within 1–2 business days. Including your device type and what you were doing when the problem happened helps us fix it faster.

Common questions

How do I get started?

Add your supplies in Inventory (with cost per unit), build a Recipe from them, log a Batch when you pour, then log Sales. Candle Lab calculates cost per candle and profit for you. The in-app guide (menu → How to use) walks through it.

Why didn't my inventory go down after a batch?

Inventory is deducted only when the batch is linked to a recipe. Edit the batch and choose a recipe, or log new batches against a recipe.

How do I cancel Pro?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google. On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Candle Lab. On Android: Google Play → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. You keep Pro until the end of the current billing period.

I got a new phone. How do I get Pro back?

Sign in with the same Candle Lab account. If Pro doesn't show, use Restore Purchases on your Profile page while signed in to the same Apple or Google account you subscribed with.

I forgot my password.

Email us from your account's email address and we'll help you get back in. If you're signed in, you can change your password anytime from your Profile page.

How do I delete my account?

Open the menu, tap your profile, then tap Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and all your data. It doesn't cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription, so cancel that separately (see above). More details are in our Privacy Policy.
