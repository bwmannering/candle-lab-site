How the wax calculator works

Why isn't an 8 oz jar 8 oz of wax?

Jars are sold by volume (fluid ounces), but wax is measured by weight (ounces on a scale). Wax is lighter than water, so an 8 fl oz jar holds about 6.9 oz of poured candle, not 8.

Where does 0.86 come from?

Soy wax weighs roughly 0.86 oz per fluid ounce of space. It's a widely used rule of thumb. Paraffin and coconut blends are a little heavier, and beeswax is heavier still, so if you use those, melt a bit more.

Why does fragrance load change the wax amount?

Fragrance oil takes up part of the jar. The calculator works out the total pour weight, then splits it into wax and oil so that the oil is your chosen percentage of the wax weight.

Should I melt extra?

Yes. Some wax always stays in the pouring pitcher, and you may need a second pour to fill sinkholes. An extra 5–10% is a good habit.
