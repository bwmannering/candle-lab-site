Pricing your candles

What does it cost to make a candle?

It depends on your supplies, but for a typical 8 oz soy candle, wax and fragrance usually come to a couple of dollars, and the jar is often the single biggest cost. Plug in your own prices above for a real number.

What's the 2× / 3× rule?

A common starting point is to charge about 2× your cost when selling wholesale to shops, and about 3× your cost for retail (selling directly to customers). It leaves room for your time, fees and profit. Adjust it for your market.

What isn't included?

This calculator covers materials. It doesn't include your time, selling fees (Etsy, card readers, market booths), shipping or packaging. Those can add up, so factor them in before you settle on a price.

How do I find my wax per candle?

Use the wax calculator: enter your jar size and 1 jar, and use the "Wax to melt" number.
