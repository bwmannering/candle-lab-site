About fragrance load

What is fragrance load?

It's how much fragrance oil you add, as a percentage of the wax weight. 1 lb of wax at 8% gets 1.28 oz of oil. Some suppliers instead measure against the finished candle's total weight, which gives a slightly lower number, so check which one your wax's instructions use.

What fragrance load should I use?

Most candle makers use 6–10%. Your wax's spec sheet lists its maximum. Going over it doesn't make a candle smell stronger. The extra oil can sweat out of the wax or burn poorly.

Should I measure fragrance oil by weight or volume?

By weight, on a scale. Fragrance oils have different densities, so a "fluid ounce" of one oil doesn't weigh the same as another.

When do I add the oil?

Follow your wax's directions. Many soy waxes call for adding oil at around 185°F (85°C) and stirring for a couple of minutes so it binds with the wax.
